Our Story

Everyday wear, finally taken seriously.

Anywear makes essentials for Indian women: comfortable, versatile, and made to fit effortlessly into real life.

The story

A few questions we asked ourselves.

Why start Anywear?

We spend most of our lives in everyday clothes, yet they're the pieces we think about the least. Anywear was created to change that.

Who is it for?

For women whose days are never just one thing: work, family, travel, meetings, celebrations, errands and moments of rest, often all in the same day.

What makes it different?

We believe everyday wear deserves the same attention as occasion wear. Every fabric, silhouette and colour is chosen with Indian women in mind.

What do we want you to feel?

Comfortable, confident and at ease wherever the day takes you, in pieces that don't need a special occasion.

What we care about

Every detail, considered.

Comfort

Every fabric is carefully chosen, so you feel at ease wherever the day takes you.

Fit

Every silhouette is designed with Indian body types in mind.

Colour

Every colour is selected to complement Indian skin tones.

From our founder
“When I started Anywear, I wasn't trying to create another clothing brand. I wanted to create clothing that women genuinely love living in.”

As women, our days are never just one thing. We move between work, family, travel, meetings, celebrations, errands, and moments of rest, often all in the same day. Yet finding clothes that are comfortable enough for real life and beautiful enough to wear confidently everywhere isn't always easy. That's where Anywear began.

Every fabric is carefully chosen, every silhouette is designed with Indian body types in mind, and every colour is selected to complement Indian skin tones. Because we believe comfort should never come at the cost of style, and style should never come at the cost of comfort.
