01

The clothes we wear most are the ones we think about least.

We spend most of our lives in everyday clothes. The outfits we reach for on busy mornings, the ones we wear through long workdays, quick coffee runs, weekend errands, family dinners and everything in between.

Yet these are the pieces we think about the least. Anywear was created to change that, because everyday wear deserves the same attention as occasion wear.

A note from our founder
“We're not here to chase trends. We're here to make the pieces you'll reach for again and again.”

Anywear began with a simple observation: everyday wear rarely gets the thought we give to occasion wear, even though it's what we wear most.

So we set out to make essentials that genuinely work for Indian women: their bodies, their skin tones, their days.
